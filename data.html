Open Science

We are committed to open, transparent science, to collaboration, and to education. Strains, data, protocols and analysis code from the MuLTEE are all here.

What is available

Strains

Any of the fifteen populations from any archived timepoint, shared on request: how a request works, what the form collects, how to name the strains you want, and where to send it.

Request strains

Genome sequences

Every public whole-genome sequencing run from the ancestor, timepoint isolates, population samples and derived strains, sortable by population, timepoint and paper, with direct links to the read files. The runs sit in four NCBI BioProjects, mainly PRJNA943273 and PRJNA1093477.

Browse the sequencing runs

Phenotype data

Phenotype measurements from the published MuLTEE papers, by population, isolate and timepoint: cluster and cell size, cell shape and packing, budding pattern, ploidy, mechanics, growth, fitness and coexisting types. Click any value to see the paper and the figure, table or passage it came from.

Browse the phenotype data

Protocols

Daily culture and settling selection, archiving and revival, transformation, DNA and RNA extraction, and phenotyping (cluster size, cell shape, volume, ploidy).

Browse the protocols

Analysis pipelines

Kai Tong's ImageJ pipelines used in the protocols: cluster segmentation for measuring cluster size, and Cellpose-based cell segmentation for measuring cell size and shape.

Browse the analysis pipelines

Everything except strains can be downloaded without an account. For data not listed, see who to write to.
