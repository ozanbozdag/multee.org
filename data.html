Data & Strains

We are developing a model system that we hope will be used by diverse researchers around the world. Strains from the frozen archive are shared on request, with no authorship requirement. We ask only that requests not overlap with work ongoing in our lab or that of a collaborator. Fill in one form and tell us what you are trying to find out.

Requesting strains

How a request works

The form tells us exactly which samples to pull from the freezer. We can currently ship within the United States only.

What the form collects

  • Recipient information, for shipping and documentation.
  • The strains you want: population, timepoint and treatment.
  • A short research plan, so we can send the protocols that go with those strains.
  • Shipping preference: overnight on dry ice, or agar stabs.

Naming what you want

Populations run PA1–5 (anaerobic), PM1–5 (mixotrophic) and PO1–5 (obligately aerobic); timepoints are written as in the papers (how to read a strain name). If you know the phenotype you want but not the timepoint, say so on the form and we will work it out.

Strains are typically shipped within two weeks of approval.

Download the request form (.docx)

Where to send it

Send the completed form to Dung T. Lac, dlac3@gatech.edu. For help choosing populations or timepoints, see Who to write to.

Data and methods

What is available

Genome sequences

Every public whole-genome sequencing run from the ancestor, timepoint isolates, population samples and derived strains, sortable by population, timepoint and paper, with direct links to the read files. The runs sit in four NCBI BioProjects, mainly PRJNA943273 and PRJNA1093477.

Browse the sequencing runs

Phenotype data

Phenotype measurements from the published MuLTEE papers, by population, isolate and timepoint: cluster and cell size, cell shape and packing, budding pattern, ploidy, mechanics, growth, fitness and coexisting types. Each value links to the figure or table it came from.

Browse the phenotype data

Protocols

Daily culture and settling selection, archiving and revival, transformation, DNA and RNA extraction, and phenotyping (cluster size, cell shape, volume, ploidy).

Browse the protocols

Analysis pipelines

Kai Tong's ImageJ pipelines used in the protocols: cluster segmentation for measuring cluster size, and Cellpose-based cell segmentation for measuring cell size and shape.

Browse the analysis pipelines
