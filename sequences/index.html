All runs (CSV)

All reads are archived in the International Nucleotide Sequence Database Collaboration (ENA, SRA, DDBJ) under BioProjects PRJNA943273. Run accessions open the ENA record; the SRA link opens the same run at NCBI.

Reads are read pairs; Files download gzipped FASTQ directly from ENA. Timepoint is the transfer the isolate was sampled at (one transfer per day). Donut and spread strains were derived from the timepoint isolate of the same line; spread strain n arose from donut strain n (Tong et al. 2025).

More runs will be added as they are deposited. To ask about data not listed here, use the request form.

The experiment

The Multicellularity Long-Term Evolution Experiment is run by the Ratcliff Lab in the School of Biological Sciences at the Georgia Institute of Technology.
